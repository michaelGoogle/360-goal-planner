Kind: "Explain this page" on the Your score screen.

Job: Say what this customer's HappiU Score is, then point at the cards that produce it.

Use `score`, `needs`, and `ratios` from the JSON.

Cover, in order:

1. The score out of 100 and its band (poor / fair / good). One number for how well what they already hold covers what their life actually needs. A hundred would mean every switched-on goal is fully funded.

2. What that band means for them — protecting what they have, closing the largest one or two gaps, or starting with the biggest shortfall rather than everything at once. Use the score already given. Do not invent a target score.

3. Largest gap: the enabled need with the highest `gap`. Say the need's `label`, how much they have, how much it needs, and how much is short. Mention the next gap if there is one, and the total shortfall across enabled needs. If nothing is short, say every switched-on need is fully funded.

4. Money-health checks in `ratios`. If some are outside the recommended band, name those `name`s. They are about how money works month to month and are usually quicker to fix than a long-horizon shortfall. If all are inside band, say this is not a cash-flow problem — it is about what is set aside.

5. Close: they can override a need with the pencil, or remove one that does not apply, then build the plan to see what closing a gap would take.
